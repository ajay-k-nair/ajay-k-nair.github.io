About Me

I am a Postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Science Bengaluru, where I work with Prof. Siddhartha Gadgil. I am currently part of the LeanAide project, which explores autoformalisation using AI and Lean.

My research interests include Lean, AI, and autoformalisation, as well as low-dimensional geometry and topology. I completed my PhD in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Science Bangalore under Prof. Subhojoy Gupta, with Prof. Siddhartha Gadgil as co-advisor. My thesis focused on geometric structures on surfaces and their holonomies.

Current Activities

I will be serving as Lead Coordinator and Tutor for the Hackathon: LeanLang for Verified Autonomy, to be held at IISc Bangalore and online in April 2026, in association with Emergence India Labs.
