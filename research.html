Research

Research Interests

My research interests include Lean, AI, and autoformalisation, as well as low-dimensional geometry and topology. I am currently contributing to the open-source project LeanAide, which aims to develop tools for translating mathematics written in natural language into Lean, an interactive theorem prover.

I also contribute to open-source Lean resources and metaprogramming material, including the Lean Metaprogramming Recipes cookbook. A broader record of my ongoing coding work and open-source contributions is available on my GitHub profile.

My interests in low-dimensional geometry and topology include geometric structures on surfaces, their holonomies, and the dynamics of mapping class group actions.

  1. Hyperbolic cone surfaces, that is, surfaces obtained by gluing hyperbolic geodesic triangles, together with their holonomies.
  2. Mapping class group actions on relative character varieties of punctured surface groups.
  3. Infinite-type surfaces and their mapping class groups.
